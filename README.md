## 1. Overview

This document records a full month of general-journal bookkeeping for Binh's Bins, a dumpster rental business, in Sage 50 Premium Accounting 2025. It covers every transaction for June 2028, from source document to posted entry, followed by the month-end adjustments, corrections, backups and financial reports. Binh's Bins is a sample company file; all screenshots are from the working file in Sage 50.

| Item | Result |
|---|---|
| Transactions recorded | 28 source documents: purchase and sales invoices, cheques, receipts, bank memos and internal memos |
| Journal entries posted | 30 (J1 to J30), including two reversing entries generated by corrections |
| New ledger accounts created | 6 (one loan, two customer and three supplier accounts) |
| Posted entries corrected | 2, using Adjust a Previously Posted Entry with a full audit trail |
| Backups taken | 5, one at the end of each working session |
| Reports produced | General Journal, Trial Balance, Income Statement, Comparative Balance Sheet, General Ledger (4 accounts), Chart of Accounts, Account List; all exported to PDF |
| Closing Trial Balance | Debits 718,610.00 = Credits 718,610.00 |

GST at 5% was recorded on every taxable purchase (2670 GST Paid on Purchases) and sale (2650 GST Charged on Services).

## 3. Journal entry procedure

Every transaction was entered in the General Journal using the same routine.

1. Set the transaction date from the calendar picker.
2. Enter the source document number in **Source** and a description (party and purpose) in **Comment**.
3. Enter each account number, then its debit or credit amount.
4. Check that the Debits and Credits totals agree, and check the GST line against 5% of the net amount.
5. Review the entry with **Report > Display General Journal Entry** where needed, then **Post**.
6. Note the journal entry number in the confirmation.
